Defining Excellence in Law & Finance

Adhitya Srinivasan is an advocate practising in Delhi, appearing before the National Company Law Appellate Tribunal, Delhi High Court, and District Court. His practice encompasses writ and constitutional matters, arbitration and alternative dispute resolution, insolvency and bankruptcy under the IBC, cheque bounce cases under the NI Act, corporate and commercial disputes, as well as criminal and civil litigation.
